1. OVERVIEW & OUR COMMITMENT TO CHILDREN'S PRIVACY

SECTION 01

First Tech Sol Global respects the privacy and safety of children and families. "First Digit: Kids Counting" (package: com.firsttechsol.firstdigit) is an educational mobile application designed for young learners (ages 2–6) to practice counting, number recognition, number tracing (0–100), basic math, and brain games.

This Privacy Policy explains what information is (and is not) handled when you use the app, how our Parent-Locked Banner Ads work, and how we comply with the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA), GDPR-K, and the Google Play Families Policy.

2. INFORMATION WE DO NOT COLLECT

SECTION 02

First Digit does NOT collect, request, sell, or share any personally identifiable information (PII) from children or parents. Specifically:

  • - No account creation, login, or registration is required.
  • - We do NOT collect names, email addresses, phone numbers, home addresses, dates of birth, photos, videos, audio recordings, or contact lists.
  • - We do NOT collect precise or approximate GPS location data.
  • - We do NOT request dangerous device permissions (no Camera, Microphone, Location, Contacts, or Photo/Storage permissions).
  • - We do NOT include chat features, social media links, public leaderboards, or multiplayer tracking.

3. BANNER-ONLY ADVERTISING & PARENT LOCK PROTECTION

SECTION 03

To keep core educational features accessible, First Digit displays ONLY banner ads. We adhere to strict child-safety protections for all ads:

A. Banner Ads Only (No Pop-Ups or Video Ads)

  • - The app displays ONLY a slim, visually separated banner ad slot powered by Google AdMob.
  • - We do NOT show full-screen interstitial pop-up ads, rewarded video ads, or app-open ads that interrupt a child's learning.

B. Protected by Parent Lock (No Direct Ad Opening by Kids)

  • - The banner ad is LOCKED BY DEFAULT on every screen.
  • - If a child accidentally taps anywhere on the ad banner, the touch is intercepted and the ad will NOT open directly.
  • - Tapping the banner instead opens a tough, multi-step Math Parental Gate (e.g., adult multiplication/addition/subtraction calculation with a numeric keypad).
  • - Only after a parent or guardian correctly solves the math challenge does the banner temporarily unlock (for 15 seconds) so an adult can inspect or open the ad if desired.
  • - If an answer is incorrect or the prompt is cancelled, the popup closes immediately and returns the child safely inside the app without opening any external link.
  • - The banner automatically re-locks when the 15-second window expires, when switching screens, when an ad opens, or when leaving and returning to the app.

C. Child-Directed & Non-Personalized Ads Only

- Google AdMob is configured with strict child-directed settings:

* Tag for Child-Directed Treatment (TFCD): Enabled
* Maximum Ad Content Rating: G (General / Family-Safe Audiences)
* Non-Personalized Ads Only (npa=1): Enabled (no behavioral profiling, no retargeting, and no interest-based advertising)

- Advertising ID & AdServices Permissions Removed:

  • * com.google.android.gms.permission.AD_ID is explicitly removed from the app manifest.
  • * Privacy Sandbox AdServices permissions (ACCESS_ADSERVICES_AD_ID, ACCESS_ADSERVICES_ATTRIBUTION, ACCESS_ADSERVICES_TOPICS) are explicitly removed from the app manifest.

- Google AdMob may process limited, non-personal technical signals (such as general IP-derived country/region for contextual delivery, device type/screen size, and coarse crash/telemetry diagnostics) strictly as permitted under Google Play Families Policy and COPPA for contextual, non-personalized banner delivery.

- For more information on how Google handles data in child-directed ads, please review Google's Privacy Policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy

4. LOCAL-ONLY DATA STORAGE & KID LOCK SECURITY

SECTION 04

All learning activity and parent controls are stored strictly locally on your device:

- Learning Progress: Stars earned, completed lessons, learned numbers, traced numbers, and game statistics are saved locally in an on-device database. This data is never uploaded to our servers.
- Audio & Voice Settings: Voice prompts and sound preferences are stored locally on the device.
- Kid Lock (PIN / Pattern): If you enable the optional Kid Lock feature in Parent Settings, your PIN or Pattern is salted and cryptographically hashed locally using PBKDF2WithHmacSHA256 and never leaves your device.

5. PERMISSIONS USED BY THE APP

SECTION 05

First Digit requests only the minimal network permissions required to load child-safe, non-personalized AdMob banner ads:

- android.permission.INTERNET
- android.permission.ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE

All core educational lessons, counting exercises, 0–100 tracing, math basics, brain games, and voice synthesis work offline on your device.

6. PARENTAL RIGHTS & DATA DELETION

SECTION 06

Because First Digit does not maintain user accounts or external servers with personal child data, you have complete control directly on your device:

- Reset Progress: Parents can clear all locally saved stars, badges, and learning history at any time inside "Kid Lock & Parent Settings" -> "Reset Child Learning Progress".
- Full Removal: Uninstalling First Digit from your device permanently deletes all locally stored progress, preferences, and Kid Lock credentials.

7. CHILDREN'S PRIVACY (COPPA / GDPR-K COMPLIANCE)

SECTION 07

We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under the age of 13 (or the applicable age of digital consent in your jurisdiction). If you are a parent or guardian and believe any unexpected information has been processed, or if you have questions about child safety in First Digit, please contact us immediately and we will promptly investigate and assist.

SECTION 08
