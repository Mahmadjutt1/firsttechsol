First Word — Kids Educational App

Learn · Explore · Grow · Ages 2 to 7 · "Big words make big dreams!"

First Word is an interactive early-childhood educational mobile application built, published, and maintained by First Tech Sol for toddlers, preschoolers, and young children (ages 2 to 7). All educational content and spoken voice pronunciations operate 100% offline.

01 · 260 A–Z Flashcards

10 Alphabet Categories

260 A–Z illustrated flashcards with voice pronunciation and sequential phonics spelling.

02 · 17 Shapes

Learn Shapes

Covers 17 geometric and everyday shapes with swipe navigation and voice audio.

03 · Trace A to Z

Dotted Letter Tracing

Dotted letter tracing from A to Z with animated directional arrow guidance.

04 · 35 Objects

Real-Life Coloring Book

35-object coloring book with region-clipped brush painting and Auto Fill Color mode.

05 · 4 Quiz Modes

Interactive Quizzes

Letter A–Z Quiz plus Easy, Medium, and Hard difficulty modes.

06 · 25 Fun Shades

Learn Colors

Covers 25 colors paired with matching real-world objects.

07 · 4-in-1 Logic Fun

4 Cognitive Brain Games

Missing Letters, 8-Card Memory Match, Pattern Train & Shadow Matcher, and Size Sorter.

08 · Screen Pinning & Gate

Parental Child-Lock Security

4-digit PIN / 3x3 pattern Screen Pinning lock and Mathematical Parental Gate.
