Dedicated Application Privacy Policy · Effective Year 2026

Privacy Policy — First Word Mobile Application

Published by First Tech Sol · www.firsttechsol.com · Google Play Families & COPPA Aligned

1. Overview & Commitment to Children's Privacy

First Tech Sol ("we", "our", or "us") developed the First Word mobile application as an educational learning environment for children featuring Alphabet categories, Learn Shapes, Start Tracing, Start Coloring, Start Quiz, Learn Colors, Brain Games, and Kid Lock security.

2. Personal Data & Account-Free Usage

First Word does not require user registration, accounts, or sign-ins. We do not directly collect or store personally identifiable information (PII) such as a child's name, email address, home address, phone number, photos, microphone recordings, or precise GPS location.

3. Advertising & Kid-Safe Ad Networks

To keep First Word free and accessible to families worldwide, the application displays contextual, family-safe advertisements (such as Google AdMob configured with child-directed treatment settings). All advertisements served within First Word adhere to child-protection standards:

  • Kids-Safe Contextual Ads Only: Ads are restricted to family-friendly, age-appropriate content and do not use interest-based behavioral tracking or remarketing.
  • Limited Technical Identifiers: Certified ad SDKs may automatically process non-personal technical data (such as device model, OS version, language, coarse country/region, and resettable advertising IDs) strictly for contextual ad delivery, frequency capping, and fraud prevention.
  • Locked External Ad Navigation: External ad buttons (such as "Visit") are protected behind First Word's parental lock mechanism to prevent accidental exits by young children.

4. Local On-Device Learning Progress

All learning progress—including completed alphabet words, letter tracing stars, coloring creations, quiz scores, and Kid Lock settings—is saved locally on your device. Uninstalling the application removes this local app data.

5. Kid Lock & Parental Security Controls

First Word includes a dedicated Kid Lock security feature on the main screen and a parent verification gate before any external link or configuration change can be accessed.

6. Contact Information

If parents or guardians have any questions regarding the First Word Privacy Policy, please contact First Tech Sol via https://www.firsttechsol.com or email privacy@firsttechsol.com.

Applies to www.firsttechsol.com & General Corporate Communications

1. Scope of This Policy

This General Services Privacy Policy describes how First Tech Sol collects, uses, and safeguards information when you visit our corporate website (https://www.firsttechsol.com) or contact our team for support, press, or business inquiries.

2. Information You Voluntarily Provide

When you contact First Tech Sol directly via email or inquiry forms, we receive the contact details and message content you choose to provide solely to respond to your inquiry and deliver requested support.

Our website is hosted statically on GitHub Pages under our custom domain (www.firsttechsol.com). Standard, non-identifying server access logs (such as browser type and requested page URL) are processed only to maintain site security, reliability, and uptime.
