Dedicated Application Privacy Policy · Effective Year 2026

Privacy Policy — First Word Mobile Application

Published by First Tech Sol · www.firsttechsol.com · COPPA & GDPR-K Aligned

1. Overview & Commitment to Children's Privacy

First Tech Sol ("we", "our", or "us") developed the First Word mobile application as a safe, self-contained educational environment for young children. We recognize the heightened obligation to protect children's privacy under the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) and global child data protection regulations.

2. Zero Personal Data Collection from Children

First Word does not require user registration, accounts, or sign-ins. The application does not collect, store, or transmit any personally identifiable information (PII), such as names, email addresses, physical addresses, phone numbers, photos, microphone recordings, or precise geolocation data.

3. Local-Only Learning Progress

All educational activity—including completed alphabet categories, letter tracing milestones, shape recognition progress, and quiz scores—is stored strictly locally on your device. Uninstalling the application permanently deletes this local data.

4. Parental Child-Lock Security Gate

First Word incorporates a dedicated Parental Child-Lock Security mechanism. Any access to parent settings or external support information requires passing an adult verification challenge, ensuring young learners cannot accidentally leave the app or alter device preferences.

5. Zero Third-Party Behavioral Advertising

First Word contains zero third-party banner ads, interstitial ads, or behavioral tracking SDKs. We never monetize children's attention or share device identifiers with advertising networks.

6. Contact Information

If parents or guardians have any questions regarding the First Word Privacy Policy, please contact First Tech Sol via https://www.firsttechsol.com or email privacy@firsttechsol.com.
