EDUCATION & EARLY LEARNING • DEBUT RELEASE • AGES 2–7

First Word

An interactive, self-contained early-childhood educational application engineered for toddlers, preschoolers, and young learners (ages 2 to 7). First Word combines spoken phonics narration, guided handwriting mechanics, creative coloring, and logic puzzles inside a 100% offline-capable, parent-protected learning sandbox.

10 Alphabet Categories (260 Cards)

260 A–Z illustrated flashcards with spoken voice pronunciation and sequential phonics spelling.

Dotted Letter Tracing (A to Z)

Interactive handwriting practice with animated directional arrow guidance for uppercase and lowercase forms.

17 Shapes & 25 Colors

Swipe-friendly geometric shapes and 25 vibrant colors paired with matching real-world objects.

35-Object Coloring Book

Real-life object outlines featuring region-clipped brush painting and instant Auto Fill Color mode.

4 Quizzes & 4 Brain Games

Easy/Medium/Hard A–Z quizzes plus Missing Letters, 8-Card Memory Match, Pattern Train & Shadow Matcher, and Size Sorter.

Screen Pinning & Math Gate

4-digit PIN / 3x3 pattern Child Lock with non-disruptive padlock tap and offline adult arithmetic recovery.

Child-Safe COPPA & GDPR-K Zero-Data Compliance: No user accounts, zero personal data collection, zero runtime device permissions (no camera, microphone, or GPS), and strictly G-rated contextual AdMob banners with AD_ID removed.

Application-Specific Privacy Standards · Tailored Data Governance

Transparent, vertical-specific privacy boundaries for every product we ship.

Because First Tech Sol builds software across distinct domains, each application is governed by a dedicated privacy architecture tailored to its audience. Our early-childhood educational titles (First Word) enforce a strict zero-personal-data offline sandbox compliant with COPPA, GDPR-K, and Google Play Families Policies. Our upcoming adult productivity and utility suites operate on local-first storage with transparent, strictly opt-in synchronization.
